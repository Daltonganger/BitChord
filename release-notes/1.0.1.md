# BitChord 1.0.1

## Search and browse

- Show up to 15 song, album, and artist suggestions while typing, up from 8.

## Requirements

- macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon.
- iOS or iPadOS 18 or later.

## Downloads and installation

- **Mac:** Apple Silicon DMG. It is ad-hoc signed, not notarized, and not Developer ID signed.
- **iOS:** Unsigned IPA for sideloading with AltStore, SideStore, or Sideloadly, or for signing with your own Apple Developer team.

### Install on Mac

Drag BitChord to Applications, then open it. If Gatekeeper blocks the first launch, use either route:

- **System Settings:** Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, choose **Open Anyway** for BitChord, then confirm **Open** in the warning dialog.
- **Terminal:** After moving BitChord to Applications, run:

  ```sh
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/BitChord.app"
  open "/Applications/BitChord.app"
  ```

### Sideload on iPhone or iPad

1. Install AltStore, SideStore, or Sideloadly and connect the device as required by that tool.
2. Import the unsigned IPA and sign it with your Apple account or developer team.
3. Install the signed app and trust the developer profile if iOS asks.

To use iCloud lyrics-choice or sign-in sharing, sign the app with your own team and enable the matching iCloud Key-Value Store and iCloud Keychain capabilities and provisioning profile. Otherwise those features use local device storage.
